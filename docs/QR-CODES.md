# Invite QR codes

Open the QR button beside the chat-list search field.

**QR Invite Code** displays your invite. Use **Copy Link** to share its text, or
let another device scan the displayed code.

**Scan QR Code** tries the default camera. Hold an invite QR code in view. Once
it is read, the camera stops and the invite appears in the text field. Review
it and select **Join** to continue.

You can also select **Open QR image** to read an image file, or **Paste QR** to
read a screenshot or invite link from the clipboard. Pasting an invite directly
into the text field also works. Image scanning supports PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP and
SVG files.

If no camera is detected, use an image or invite link. **Use camera** retries
camera access after you connect a device. An unreadable image or an image
without a QR code produces an error and clears the previous scanned invite.

Scanned codes are not joined automatically. Core validates the invite when you
select **Join**. Switching to the invite tab or closing the dialog stops camera
capture.

This feature is experimental. See the [compatibility tracker](COMPATIBILITY.md)
for current validation and remaining hardware checks.
